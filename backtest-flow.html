System map · research only reads, never writes

How backtesting flows into the dashboard

Two lanes are drawn below. The top lane is how a live call gets made: the market facts are collected and saved, five separate robots each write one 24-hour call, and Layer 2 combines them. The bottom lane is backtesting: it works on a copy of what was already saved, re-runs the old rules over it, and shows the result as evidence. The two lanes meet only on this dashboard.

Lane A · live calls

Where the live calls come from, every refresh

Five robots, one per asset. Each one reads only its own rulebook and the newest usable facts, then posts one call. Nothing here reads the backtesting side, and no robot sees another robot's answer.

  1. 01 · COLLECT Collectors (n8n) Six collectors (Eco Events plus one each for USD, EUR, Gold, NQ and BTC) fetch the market facts and save one snapshot per refresh. saves market_snapshots
  3. 02 · STORE Supabase market_snapshots The one saved set of market facts. The live robots read it now, and backtesting copies it later. saved market facts
  5. 03 · LAYER 1 Five sealed agents Each robot reads only its own rulebook and the newest usable facts, then posts one call: up or down over the next 24 hours. saves agent_outputs
  7. 04 · STORE Supabase agent_outputs Those calls saved exactly as written, kept apart for each asset. saved calls
  9. 05 · LAYER 2 Pair selection Built on top of Layer 1: it reads the five finished calls and adds one extra call when two assets point opposite ways. It only combines what it was given; it never recalculates a call. saves layer2.json
  11. 06 · PUBLISH Dashboard Writer → GitHub Pages Writes the files this dashboard reads: the five calls, the Layer 2 call and the job status. → Layer 1 tiles · Layer 2 grid
Read-only boundary the one link between the two lanes, and it only goes one way

Backtesting copies the saved tables, the market history and the rulebooks as they stood. Production never reads a research artifact, so nothing a backtest does can change a live call, a workflow or a saved row.

Research may read
agent_outputs · market_snapshots · the rulebooks as they stood · registries · market history
Research may never write
n8n workflows · Supabase runtime tables · live call path · another robot's input

Lane B · backtesting (reads a copy only)

How a backtest earns the right to be shown

Six steps, always in this order. A step is only believed once the step before it reproduces what the live system actually did on that date.

  1. 01 · COPY Frozen inputs A copy of the saved calls, the market history, the registries and the rulebooks exactly as they stood. Nothing live is touched. copy taken, nothing touched
  3. 02 · REBUILD Snapshot builders Rebuilds the exact facts the robot saw at that moment, and says honestly where a fact was missing or only a stand-in. match the live run first
  5. 03 · REPLAY Replay runners Re-runs the old rulebook one asset at a time, on the copied facts. Assets are never mixed, and a replay has to match what the live run did before its results count. same rules, new dates
  7. 04 · SCORE Outcome evaluation Checks what the price actually did over the next 24 hours. A call only counts as right if the move beat the market's ordinary noise. right call vs normal noise
  9. 05 · CHECK Checker artifacts An automatic checker has to pass with no failures and nothing missing, and whatever it could not cover is written down next to the result. evidence + limits
  11. 06 · PUBLISH Research artifacts Saved as fixed, dated files that cannot change quietly, next to the pages that read them. data/*.json

Two lanes, one screen: the research sits beside the live call, never inside it.

Reading the lines

What this page does say

  • One live chain: the collectors save snapshots, five separate robots each write one call, Layer 2 combines those finished calls, and the Dashboard Writer puts them on the page.
  • One research chain: backtesting copies saved data and the old rules, replays them, scores them, checks them, and saves the result as evidence.
  • One crossing, one direction: research reads production. Production reads no research, and no Layer 1 robot reads another robot, Layer 2, or a research file.

Keep the picture honest

What this page does not say

  • Not proof of skill: a replay that matches the past only shows the old rules were followed on old data. It is not proof that the calls cause anything, are accurate, or make money.
  • Not a live change: a file exported on this machine, or a test passing here, does not mean anything in the live system changed.
  • Not complete: where a date or a value is only a stand-in for the real thing, the gap is written down on the research pages instead of being smoothed over.
