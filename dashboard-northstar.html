What we are building, why it matters, and what good looks like.

This page keeps the dashboard effort pointed at one mission: a trading-grade interface that makes live calls, research context, and operational health immediately legible without inventing certainty the models have not earned.

Current work

Shift the dashboard into a trading-terminal visual language

We are making the interface feel closer to a live market workstation: darker canvas, denser signal framing, sharper contrast, clearer state badges, and calmer separation between live and research-only surfaces.

Why now

Clarity needs to lead confidence

The system already contains live calls, pair analysis, workflow telemetry, and frozen research. A more disciplined visual hierarchy helps us understand what is actionable, what is context only, and what remains explicitly non-defensible.

North star

Become the trustworthy read layer for sealed model output

The goal is not cosmetic flash. The goal is a dashboard that feels like a serious trading instrument: fast to scan, difficult to misread, and honest about the boundary between production outputs and research artifacts.

What we optimize for

Design priorities

  • Immediate scanability for active calls, confidence, and workflow state.
  • Market-terminal atmosphere without sacrificing readability.
  • Consistent visual cues for bullish, bearish, warning, and neutral states.
  • Research honesty: frozen findings should look authoritative but never live.

What we avoid

Non-goals

  • Pretty-but-vague visuals that hide workflow truth.
  • Luxury or editorial styling that feels detached from trading context.
  • Research presentation that overstates predictive validity.
  • Visual complexity that makes the live call hierarchy harder to read.
