North Star

Does one independent USD call at 05:00 London, built from the 25-factor research, improve on our current model? We measure four things: ① DXY directional accuracy, ② how often EUR/USD, gold, BTC/USD and NAS100 move opposite the USD call, ③ full-level L2L trading results at 5R, and ④ half-level L2L trading results at 3R. Both trade simulations start at £100,000 and risk 1% of the account balance per filled trade.

Research only. Neither model is published as a live call, and no figure here is an edge claim.
