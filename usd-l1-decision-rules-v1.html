Eligibility is the binding constraint, not the direction. 404 of 725 mornings failed the RG-4 volatility gate (373 closed, 31 undefined), a further 1 open morning had too little of the book, 124 eligible mornings sat inside the 0.20 deadband, and 196 produced a direction. Of those, 87 were bullish and 109 bearish. A no-call is never filled or converted to a flat vote. These are replayed decisions under version 1, not the stored challenger's published calls.

The decision, in order

The research fixes seven groups and one unit of say per family, but it never names the families, which factors vote, a combination rule, a band set or a gate. Version 1 chooses 12 voting signals across six voting families, keeps the seventh group as a non-voting quality gate, and applies these steps in order.

01

Vote inside each family. A signal votes +1 for USD strength, −1 for USD weakness, or 0. A signal with no value at the cutoff abstains; it is never replaced by zero, a later print or a neighbouring session. The family vote is the arithmetic mean of its usable signal votes.

02

Weight the families equally. 6 families each cast one vote regardless of how many signals they contain, so rates cannot outvote commodities by having more columns. The quality gate does not vote.

03

Score agreement and participation. Both are needed because a book can agree strongly while most of it is absent.

net score = Σ family votes
agreement = |net score| ÷ usable families
participation = usable voting signals ÷ 12
04

Apply the gate. A direction needs at least 6 usable signals, at least 4 usable families, and an RG-4 volatility gate that is defined and open. The event family must also be usable for the provisional trade rule, because a direction read without the event family is incomplete.

05

Apply the deadband. A morning inside 0.20 agreement is a recorded no-call, not a coin toss and not a flat position.

06

Place the band, then cap it. A missing family caps the result at band 3. An unstaged event calendar caps it at band 4. Caps are recorded with the morning rather than silently reducing the score.

07

Answer the trade question. The provisional trade rule is met only at band 3 or above, with the RG-4 gate open, the event family usable, and all twelve signals usable. No sizing, entry, stop or execution is defined in this version.

Where 725 mornings went

What this page does not claim

  • Research only. No figure on this page is an edge claim, and no call is published as live.
  • The rules are frozen by judgement before this version's accuracy was evaluated; they are not fitted to the 725 mornings.
  • A missing factor is abstained on, never filled with zero or a neighbouring print.
  • The event calendar is not staged, so band 5 cannot be reached and every band-4 result carries that cap.
  • F8 is specified and computed from its archive for the worked morning, but run-v1.json does not yet read it; the published challenger remains the nine-input experiment.
  • The band-3 trade threshold is inherited from the nine-input book. It is a research floor, not a validated gate.

The worked morning is an arithmetic demonstration, not a result; it was not selected for its outcome. The scored section reports hit counts and their intervals, and nothing else: no return, Sharpe, drawdown or comparison with the deployed model, no out-of-sample split, and no claim that a hit count above one half is an edge. The conviction bands are in-sample and band 5 never occurred. F8 is the one signal read outside the session row: it is reconstructed from the Fed statement archive by release time, not from a stored receipt.
