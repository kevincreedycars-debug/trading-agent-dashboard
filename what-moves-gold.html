Gold · what moves the price

What moves the gold price

Which things influence the gold price, which way each one pushes it, and how big a part of the picture it is - in one table, for a reader who has never opened a research page.

As of 4 October 2026 Back to the Gold page
These directions are the system's own written expectations, not results.

They are the rules the gold agent works to. The archive has been scored against them and has not produced a usable direction, so read every row as "this is what the system expects, and why" rather than as a promise. The measured verdict lives on the Direction tab of the Gold page.

The ten factors

The weight is the share of the picture the system gives a factor, out of 100. It is not a probability, not a confidence and not a forecast.

The top four rows are the system's own primary drivers: when they disagree it lowers its confidence in the call, and when they agree it raises it.

Where the number 28 comes from

The gold agent is allowed to read 28 declared readings. They are not 28 separate ideas: ten of them are the ten factors above, and the rest are the levels, changes and event fields those factors are read from. The declaration itself puts it this way - ten weighted factors plus explicit event decomposition - and twenty of the twenty-eight are levels or transformations of five market series, so they are not twenty independent inputs.

One honest caveat travels with the dollar rows: the series the live agent reads is a broad trade-weighted dollar index, not the classic ICE dollar index, so the row says "how strong the dollar is" rather than naming a contract.

What the archive has shown about these directions

  • The directions above are the agent's declared expectations, written by the system, not findings from the data.
  • The archive has been scored against them and returned no usable direction: on the daily read all 25 scored states came back with the same verdict, no information; on the weekly read 24 of 25 did, and one was marked unstable across years.
  • Exactly one daily row reached the level the system calls interesting, against roughly two and a half rows that would land there by chance among the rows tested. That is the noise floor, not an edge.

Those figures are the ones the Direction tab of the Gold page already publishes. Nothing new is measured here.

What this page is not

It is not a forecast, a pick, a ranking, a score, a signal or a trading result. It does not claim that any row works, it does not replace the measured verdict on the Direction tab, and it answers one question only: what influences the gold price, which way, and how much of the picture it is.

Built from the system's own written rules and its declared reading list, both already in the project. No market data was read or recalculated to make this page, no figure on it is new to the site, and nothing on it is advice.
