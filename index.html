Overview Definitions

What the numbers mean

BullishThe model expects the asset is more likely to move higher over the selected timeframe.
BearishThe model expects the asset is more likely to move lower over the selected timeframe.
Bullish LeanThe evidence currently favours a bullish move, but the advantage over the bearish case is not strong enough for a full Bullish call. Treat this as a developing upside bias, not a high-conviction signal.
Bearish LeanThe evidence currently favours a bearish move, but the advantage over the bullish case is not strong enough for a full Bearish call. Treat this as a developing downside bias, not a high-conviction signal.
ConfidenceA 0-100 composite score showing how strongly the available evidence supports the selected direction. It combines directional evidence, weighted participation, and the size of the directional edge, then applies penalties for missing data, weak separation, event risk, and other reliability conditions. It is a conviction score, not a guaranteed probability of success.
Bull CaseThe percentage of active directional evidence supporting an upward or bullish call.
Bear CaseThe percentage of active directional evidence supporting a downward or bearish call.
Net EdgeThe gap between bull evidence and bear evidence. A wider gap means the active evidence is less conflicted.
ParticipationThe weighted share of expected model evidence that supplied valid, usable directional input for the call. Higher participation means more of the intended weighted inputs contributed directional evidence. Low participation reduces confidence because the call rests on a thinner active evidence base.
StrengthA plain-language classification derived from the final confidence score after all penalties and reliability checks, with minimum net-edge and participation gates for the stronger Layer 1 labels. It summarises model conviction as Weak, Moderate, Strong, or Very Strong, not the size of the expected market move.
No CallThe model did not have enough clean evidence, participation, or market conditions to issue a directional call.
